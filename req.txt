Using a deep learning framework: Autoencoders
Due 22 Nov by 23:59 Points 10 Submitting a text entry box or a file upload Available 11 Nov at 16:00 - 22 Nov at 23:59
This week, you will do what you were hoping for last week: use someone else's implementation of backpropagation.

You will build a convolutional autoencoder using one of the deep learning libraries that you can find online.  You will investigate if it is possible to use a (simple) autoencoder to colourise grayscale images.

As last week, you will solve this assignment in pairs. Please create the pair on the People/Assignment pairs page in Canvas (if you did not do it for the first assignment) and clearly indicate who you partnered up with when you submit your results (include your names in the report and add a comment in the submission indicating the authors).
